THE ARTIST / BEHIND THE FRAME

Tarynn Lee

I am a quiet, thoughtful person who prefers to observe and feel before putting ideas into words. Through photography, video editing and visual design, I record small details and moments from everyday life. I hope to build this body of work with care, valuing every attempt and lesson, and share these creations when the time feels right.

I am currently learning to create AI videos with Wonderclip, exploring image generation, moving-image storytelling and visual expression. This is an ongoing learning journey, not a claim of finished professional expertise.
