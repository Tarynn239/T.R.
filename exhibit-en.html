EXHIBIT NO. 001 / THE IMAGE IN WAITING

Where the Light Falls

Beginning at the boundary between light and shadow, this piece notices details that are easy to overlook. Rather than offering an immediate answer, the image invites viewers to pause and bring their own memories and feelings into the frame—a study of looking, space and time.
