TABLE

A furniture design created in Shapr3D. The concept combines a segmented tabletop, a contrasting central inset and a layered base, balancing visual character with everyday utility.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

From Sketch to 3D Model

The project explores a modern, practical coffee table with distinctive tabletop geometry, clean lines and balanced proportions suited to contemporary living spaces.

DESIGN PROCESS

  1. Sketching — Created the tabletop outline with the Sketch tool.
  2. Building the solid form — Used Extrude to turn the 2D sketch into a 3D solid.
  3. Refining dimensions and edges — Adjusted height, width and support thickness, then rounded edges with Fillet.
  4. Assembly and materials — Combined the tabletop and base, applied wood texture and color, and reviewed proportions for presentation.

DESIGN CONSIDERATIONS

The project document also considers durability, efficient material use and reducing production waste. Teak or reclaimed wood, water-based finishes and easy assembly are proposed as sustainability directions; these are design considerations, not verified material-test results.
