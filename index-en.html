A PERSONAL ARCHIVE IN PROGRESS

Slowly observed,
slowly taking shape.

I am a quiet, thoughtful person who prefers to observe and feel before putting ideas into words. Through photography, video editing and visual design, I collect small details and moments. I hope to build this body of work with care, valuing every experiment and lesson, and share it when the time feels right.
